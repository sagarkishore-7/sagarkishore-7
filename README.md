## `01 //` whoami

I build the pipelines that turn raw signal into decisions — OSINT into tracked incidents, telemetry into detections, and unstructured reports into schema-validated intelligence.

Three years across **threat intelligence**, **detection engineering** and **security automation**: most recently a CTI researcher at **CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security**, and before that on email security at **1&1 Mail & Media**, tuning classification and detection across infrastructure carrying roughly **4 billion messages a week**.

<td width="50%" valign="top">

Multi-source ingestion with schema-constrained LLM extraction into a typed CTI schema, deterministic multi-signal canonicalisation, and blind expert validation of the classifier. *Details available on request while the work is under peer review.*

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

<a href="https://github.com/sagarkishore-7/LLMMap"><img alt="LLMMap" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sagarkishore-7/sagarkishore-7/main/assets/cards/pin-LLMMap.svg" width="100%"></a>

Prompt injection testing framework for LLM-integrated applications. Dual-LLM attack-and-judge architecture covering **227 injection techniques across 18 attack families**, with **SARIF v2.1.0** export so results drop straight into CI/CD. Ships with PromptLab, a vulnerable/defended simulation lab mapped to the OWASP LLM Top 10.
